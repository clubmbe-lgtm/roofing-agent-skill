# On the roof: what to know before quoting

Ask only what is still missing, one question at a time, most important
first.

1. **Size**: how many squares (100 sq ft each) the work covers.
2. **Pitch**: the slope, as rise over 12 (a 6/12 roof rises 6 inches per
   foot). Steep roofs take longer and need more safety gear.
3. **Layers**: how many layers of shingles come off before the new ones go
   on.
4. **Decking**: soft or rotten spots in the boards under the shingles, and
   roughly how many sheets to replace.
5. **Penetrations**: vents, pipes, chimney and skylights to flash around.
6. **Edges**: drip edge, gutters and downspouts, and whether they stay.
7. **Ventilation**: ridge vent or box vents, and whether the attic breathes.
8. **Access**: where the dumpster and the delivery truck can park.
9. **Permit**: whether the job needs one where the house is.

Write down what they answer in their words; the quote uses those words.
